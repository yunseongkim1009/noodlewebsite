8:02 pmHow a question works

He won't fetch until you throw.

Try it here. This is the practice chat with canned answers. On your Mac, Noodle does the same over the real AI apps and websites.

  1. You ask

    Press Enter in an AI chat and Noodle trots over with his ball. He lays a sheet over the answer.

  2. You have a go

    Write what you already think. Rough notes, one idea per line. Skip is always there, and counts as outsourced.

  3. He chases the ball

    The ball flies off, he runs after it, and the answer finishes loading while he's gone.

  4. He brings back the gap

    The sheet lifts. You see what you got on your own, what you missed, and what only you said. Every missed point becomes a review card.

Practice chat
Why did the Roman Republic fall?
  • Economic inequality. Conquest made a few families very rich while small farmers lost their land.
  • Powerful generals. Marius, Sulla and Pompey led armies that were loyal to them, not to the state.
  • Political violence. The killing of the Gracchi brothers made murder an accepted political tool.
  • Civil wars. Rival generals fought each other for control of Rome.
  • Julius Caesar. He crossed the Rubicon, won the civil war and was named dictator for life.
